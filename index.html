01 / A little about me

Grounded in detail.
Looking at the whole.

Across 23 global engagements at Deloitte USI, I moved from audit testing to owning critical financial statement areas and independently managing delivery for a large public company. My work spanned five geographies and industries including technology, media, telecommunications and renewable energy.

I learned to find the signal in dense information, challenge assumptions with evidence and help stakeholders reach decisions. I have redirected audit effort toward higher-risk areas, shortened issue resolution from about a week to 48 hours, and led teams through high-pressure deadlines.

I am pursuing the IPMX programme at IIM Lucknow to build on that foundation and move closer to strategy and transformation work. I bring analytical discipline, practical judgement and a strong sense of ownership.
